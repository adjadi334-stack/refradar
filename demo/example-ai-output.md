# What ChatGPT gave me for my thesis (typical output — do not trust it blindly)

According to the seminal transformer paper [Attention Is All You Need](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762),
self-attention replaced recurrence. A later replication (https://arxiv.org/abs/9999.99999) refined it.

The deep learning review by LeCun, Bengio and Hinton (DOI 10.1038/nature14539) remains the
canonical introduction. A 2024 mega-analysis, published as DOI 10.1234/refradar.fake.9.9.9,
reportedly confirms these findings "beyond any reasonable doubt".

Clinically, evidence can be found in PubMed under PMID 33301246, while a contradictory
preprint lives at https://example.com/definitely-not-a-real-study-refradar.

Background reading: ISBN 978-0-262-03384-8 (valid checksum — Introduction to Algorithms) and
the often-cited ISBN 978-2-07-000000-0 (invalid checksum — a classic copy-paste artefact).
